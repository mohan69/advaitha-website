# RightSense Ecosystem Brand System

## Brand hierarchy

- RightSense Technologies — parent company, AI-native enterprise transformation, integration and outcome delivery.
- Advaitha — Enterprise AI Architecture & Advisory.
- PulseIQ — Enterprise Operating Intelligence Platform.
- WinsProposal — Revenue and Proposal Intelligence Platform.
- TalentPulse — AI-Native Talent Intelligence Platform.

## Canonical ecosystem proposition

RightSense Technologies helps enterprises turn fragmented data, processes and decisions into measurable business outcomes through enterprise AI architecture, transformation expertise and three purpose-built intelligence platforms.

Use this hierarchy consistently in public-facing copy:

- Advaitha defines enterprise AI strategy, architecture, governance and decision boundaries.
- RightSense provides transformation, integration and delivery capability.
- PulseIQ, WinsProposal and TalentPulse accelerate specific operating outcomes where there is a clear fit.

Do not position RightSense itself as the Enterprise Operating Intelligence Platform; that role belongs to PulseIQ.

## Typography

Use Inter only for web UI, with sensible system fallbacks.

## Palette

- Primary ink: `#0f172a`
- Dark hero: `#102a4c`
- Brand blue: `#2f60f5`
- Deep brand blue: `#1c47d8`
- Teal accent: `#0ea5a4`
- Canvas: `#f8fafc`
- Border: `#e2e8f0`
- White: `#ffffff`

Preserve each site’s extended token scale, card radii, shadows, spacing system, and light/dark grid language when extending the design.

## Product naming

Never abbreviate or rename the canonical products in public-facing copy.

## Outcome vocabulary

Prefer revenue, margin, cash / working capital, productivity, execution speed, risk reduction, decision clarity, decision velocity and verified business value.

## Canonical product descriptions

- PulseIQ — Connects operational evidence across ERP, CRM, CPQ, engineering, planning and execution to show what is putting revenue, margin and customer commitments at risk, what is causing it, what can still be recovered and whether the resulting action created verified business value.
- WinsProposal — Turns proposals, RFP responses and pricing decisions into a learning loop that improves win rate, proposal velocity and margin protection.
- TalentPulse — Connects talent acquisition, workforce capacity, skills, readiness and productivity signals so leaders can see whether the organization has the people and capability to deliver its commitments.

## Entry motions

Route the customer to the smallest engagement capable of proving value:

- Enterprise AI strategy, architecture or governance uncertainty → Advaitha Executive AI Opportunity & Architecture Assessment.
- Enterprise-wide operating-intelligence uncertainty → RightSense 48-Hour Diagnostic.
- Order, revenue, margin or execution risk → PulseIQ Outcome Discovery / representative-order proof.
- Proposal, RFP or pricing performance → WinsProposal discovery / pilot.
- Hiring, staffing, skills, capacity or workforce-readiness problems → TalentPulse discovery / demo.

Do not claim that every customer must start with the RightSense 48-Hour Diagnostic.

## Experience rules

Across public sites maintain consistency in typography, primary colors, buttons, cards, spacing, dark/light surfaces, product descriptions, ecosystem links, outcome terminology, CTA routing and social metadata.

## Role separation

Advaitha must never be positioned as another software platform. It is the Enterprise AI Architecture & Advisory practice within the RightSense Technologies ecosystem.

## Change management

Any future public-site change affecting one ecosystem brand should be checked against this document before merge.
