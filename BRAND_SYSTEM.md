# RightSense Ecosystem Brand System

## Brand hierarchy

- RightSense Technologies — parent company, enterprise transformation and delivery.
- Advaitha — Enterprise AI Architecture & Advisory.
- PulseIQ — Enterprise Operating Intelligence Platform.
- WinsProposal — Revenue and Proposal Intelligence Platform.
- TalentPulse — AI-Native Talent Intelligence Platform.

## Typography

Use Inter only for web UI, with sensible system fallbacks.

## Palette

- Primary ink: `#0f172a`
- Dark hero: `#102a4c`
- Brand blue: `#2f60f5`
- Deep brand blue: `#1c47d8`
- Teal accent: `#0ea5a4`
- Canvas: `#f8fafc`
- Border: `#e2e8f0`
- White: `#ffffff`

Preserve each site’s extended token scale, card radii, shadows, spacing system, and light/dark grid language when extending the design.

## Product naming

Never abbreviate or rename the canonical products in public-facing copy.

## Outcome vocabulary

Prefer revenue, margin, cash / working capital, productivity, execution speed, risk reduction, decision clarity, and decision velocity.

## Experience rules

Across public sites maintain consistency in typography, primary colors, buttons, cards, spacing, dark/light surfaces, product descriptions, ecosystem links, outcome terminology, and social metadata.

## Role separation

Advaitha must never be positioned as another software platform. It is the Enterprise AI Architecture & Advisory practice within the RightSense Technologies ecosystem.

## Change management

Any future public-site change affecting one ecosystem brand should be checked against this document before merge.
